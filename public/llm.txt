We supply IS 383 graded, double-washed materials to homeowners, masons, contractors and civil engineers — with same-day tipper delivery to Tambaram, Velachery, OMR, ECR, Ambattur, Avadi and Anna Nagar.

## Products
- [All 9 Grades](https://oneinfra.in/products): M-Sand (Double Washed), P-Sand (Plastering Sand), Crusher Dust, 12mm Chips, 20mm Jelly Stones, 20mm Blue Metal (Grade A), 25mm Aggregate, 40mm Gravel (HBG), WMM (Wet Mix Macadam)
- [M-Sand (Double Washed)](https://oneinfra.in/products#m-sand-double-washed): Crushed granite fines, double-washed to IS 383 Zone II/III. ₹1,250/ton. Default for Chennai RCC and plaster.
- [P-Sand (Plastering Sand)](https://oneinfra.in/products#p-sand): Fine-grade double-washed plastering sand, IS 1542 graded. ₹1,400/ton. For smooth wall and ceiling finishes.
- [20mm Jelly Stones](https://oneinfra.in/products#20mm-jelly): Standard 16-20 mm coarse aggregate for RCC slabs, beams and columns. ₹1,150/ton.
- [20mm Blue Metal (Grade A)](https://oneinfra.in/products#20mm-blue-metal): Tight-graded clean blue metal for high-grade M25+ concrete. ₹1,200/ton.
- [Crusher Dust](https://oneinfra.in/products#crusher-dust): Fine blue-metal dust (<4.75 mm) for sub-base, backfill and compaction. ₹950/ton.
- [12mm Chips](https://oneinfra.in/products#12mm-chips): 10-12 mm blue-metal chips for floor screed, thin RCC, pathway gravel. ₹1,100/ton.
- [25mm Aggregate](https://oneinfra.in/products#25mm-blue-metal): 20-25 mm coarse aggregate for mass concrete and road sub-base. ₹1,250/ton.
- [40mm Gravel (HBG)](https://oneinfra.in/products#40mm-blue-metal): 40-63 mm large aggregate for foundation backfill and drainage. ₹1,200/ton.
- [WMM (Wet Mix Macadam)](https://oneinfra.in/products#wmm): Pre-mixed graded aggregate with controlled moisture for road base. ₹1,100/ton.
